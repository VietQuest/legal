Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

VietQuest ("we," "our," or "us") is a mobile app that helps people learn Vietnamese. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use VietQuest, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the app, you agree to the practices described here.

How We Use Information

  • To save and sync your learning progress across sessions (Premium only)
  • To let any learner sign in, join the optional weekly leaderboard, and restore a Premium account
  • To show your chosen display name and weekly score to other signed-in learners on the leaderboard
  • To receive, read, and respond to feedback you choose to send us, and occasionally reward especially helpful feedback with bonus gems at our discretion
  • To show ads that help keep the app free, and to measure ad performance
  • To maintain and improve the app's features and content

Weekly Leaderboard

Signing in (with email or Google) is optional and free for every learner — it is not limited to Premium subscribers. If you sign in, VietQuest may show your weekly correct-answer count and a display name on a leaderboard visible to other signed-in learners. By default this display name is a shortened, partly-masked version of your email (for example, "van***"); you can change it to anything you like from Settings. The leaderboard resets every week, and only your current week's score is shown. You can stop appearing on the leaderboard at any time by signing out.

Feedback

If you send feedback through the app, we store your message together with your account ID so we can read it and, if useful, follow up or improve the app. Feedback is never displayed to other users. We may, at our sole discretion, reward a learner with bonus gems for a particularly helpful submission — this is not guaranteed and is not an entitlement for any specific feedback.

Advertising & Tracking

By default, VietQuest requests non-personalized ads. On iOS, the app asks for App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission once, the first time you encounter an ad. If you are in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, VietQuest shows a consent form (via Google's User Messaging Platform) so you can choose whether to allow personalized ads before any ad is requested.

Children's Privacy

VietQuest is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under that age.

Data Retention & Deletion

  • Local learning data can be erased at any time using the "Reset Progress" option in the app.
  • Cloud account data, leaderboard entries, and feedback can be deleted by emailing us; we will delete it within 30 days.
  • We do not retain advertising identifiers ourselves — they are handled directly by Google AdMob.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct or delete your personal data
  • Withdraw consent for personalized ads at any time
  • Object to or restrict certain processing of your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.
