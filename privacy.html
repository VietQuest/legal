Privacy Policy for VietQuest

Effective date: September 19, 2026

VietQuest ("the App", "we", "us") is developed and operated by an independent individual developer. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using VietQuest, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Locally stored data (all users)

Your learning progress — streaks, completed levels, scores, settings, and similar data — is stored directly on your device using on-device storage. This data is never transmitted to us unless you choose to enable Cloud Backup (see 1.2).

1.2 Account and cloud sync data (Premium users who opt in)

If you are a Premium subscriber and choose to sign in with Google to enable Cloud Backup, we collect:

  • Your Google account email address and display name (via Firebase Authentication), used solely to identify your account and restore your progress on other devices.
  • Your learning progress data (streaks, completed levels, scores, gems, weekly stats, and similar fields), stored via Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) so it can sync across your devices.

Cloud sync is entirely optional. If you do not sign in, none of this data ever leaves your device.

1.3 Voice recordings (Tone Match feature)

If you use the "Tone Match" pronunciation practice feature, your microphone audio is analyzed entirely on your device. Voice recordings are never uploaded to us or to any third-party server, and are not stored after analysis completes.

1.4 Advertising data (free-tier users)

Free-tier (non-Premium) users may see ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, or Android Advertising ID) to serve and measure ads. On iOS, we request your permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework before any such identifier is used; if you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized. Premium subscribers never see ads and this data is never collected from them.

We do not use Facebook SDK, Google Analytics, or any other behavioral analytics or tracking tool beyond what is described above.

1.5 Automatically collected technical data

Like most online services, our service providers (Google Firebase, Google AdMob) automatically record certain technical information in server logs when the App communicates with their servers — such as IP address, device/OS type, and app version. This is standard technical logging used for operating, securing, and troubleshooting the service, and is not used to build an advertising profile of you beyond what is described in section 1.4.

2. How We Use Information

  • To let Premium subscribers back up and restore their learning progress across devices.
  • To operate and measure the AdMob advertising shown to free-tier users.
  • To provide customer support when you contact us.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone, and we do not use your learning progress or voice data for advertising purposes.

3. Who We Share Data With

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore) — our cloud infrastructure provider for account sign-in and progress sync.
  • Google AdMob — our advertising provider for free-tier users, subject to Google's own privacy policy.
  • Apple / Google Play — for processing subscription payments; VietQuest itself never sees or stores your payment card details.

We do not share your data with any other third party, and we do not sell it.

3.1 International data transfer

Our service providers (Google Firebase, Google AdMob) may process and store data on servers located outside your country of residence, including in the United States or other countries where Google operates data centers. By using the App, you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in countries that may have different data protection laws than your own.

4. Data Retention, Deletion, and Your Rights

Locally stored data remains on your device until you delete the app. Cloud-synced data remains linked to your account until you request deletion.

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you (your synced progress and account email).
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Delete your cloud account data.
  • Withdraw consent to cloud sync at any time by signing out in Settings.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at the address below — we will respond and act on your request within a reasonable time.

5. Children's Privacy

VietQuest is not directed or marketed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. Premium subscriptions and Cloud Backup accounts are intended to be created and managed by an adult (e.g. a parent using their own Google account). If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child under 13 without appropriate consent, we will delete it promptly. If you believe this has happened, please contact us at the email below.

6. Security

We use industry-standard practices (including Firebase Security Rules and encrypted connections) to protect the data we hold. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices

  • You can use most of VietQuest's features entirely offline and without creating an account.
  • You can sign out of Cloud Backup at any time in Settings.
  • On iOS, you can change your App Tracking Transparency permission at any time in your device's Settings app.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective date" above. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
