Privacy Policy for VietQuest

Effective date: October 15, 2026

VietQuest ("the App", "we", "us") is developed and operated by an independent individual developer. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using VietQuest, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Locally stored data (all users)

Your learning progress — streaks, completed levels, scores, settings, and similar data — is stored directly on your device using on-device storage. This data is never transmitted to us unless you choose to create an account (see 1.3). The free version needs an internet connection to run its lessons and ads; Premium subscribers can also use the App offline. To show your daily practice reminder, the App can schedule a local notification on your device; this reminder is created and delivered by your device, and no notification token or push-notification data is sent to us.

1.2 Age screening (all users)

The first time you open VietQuest, we ask what year you were born. This is used only to determine, on your device, whether you are under 13 — nothing more precise than that is ever asked or recorded. Your birth year itself is never transmitted to us or stored anywhere; only the resulting under-13/13-or-older result is saved, locally on your device, so the App can remember your answer the next time you open it. If you tell us you're under 13, VietQuest automatically: disables account creation and sign-in (see 1.3) so no email or other account data is ever collected from you, and switches advertising to Google's child-directed mode, which turns off ad personalization and restricts ad content (see 1.7). Users who installed VietQuest before this screening existed were not asked and are not affected retroactively.

1.3 Account and cloud sync data (optional, 13 and older only)

Most of VietQuest's learning content can be used without ever creating an account. If you choose to create an account — by signing in with Google, signing in with Apple, or registering with an email address and password — we collect and store the following via Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore):

  • Your email address and/or display name, used to identify your account, let you sign back in, and (for email/password accounts) verify your email.
  • A unique account ID (User ID), used internally to link your data to your account.
  • Your learning progress data (streaks, completed levels, scores, gems, weekly stats, and similar fields), so it can sync across your devices. Cloud sync of progress is a Premium feature.
  • An optional nickname you set, shown publicly on the in-app Leaderboard alongside your score. If you do not set one, a default name made from the first three letters of your email address followed by *** is shown instead.
  • A device identifier, used only to enforce a limit on how many devices can be signed into one account at the same time (to prevent account sharing/abuse).
  • Referral program data: your referral code, a code you redeem, the number of friends who joined with your code, the nickname of each invited friend (shortened to at most 10 characters) shown in your list of invited friends, and markers recording whether the gem rewards were paid. We use this to give both you and the invited friend 50 gems once the friend finishes 3 new lessons, and to prevent the same reward from being paid twice. Referral gems are an in-app reward with no cash value; there is a limit on rewarded invites per account.
  • Any message you submit through the in-app Feedback form, together with your account ID, so we can respond to it.

Signing in and creating an account is entirely optional, and is not available at all if you told us you're under 13 (see 1.2). If you never sign in, none of this data ever leaves your device.

1.4 Purchase data (Premium subscribers)

If you subscribe to VietQuest Premium, our subscription management provider, RevenueCat, receives and stores your purchase and subscription status (e.g. active/expired, renewal date) together with your account ID, so the app can verify your Premium entitlement across devices. VietQuest itself never sees or stores your payment card details — these are handled entirely by Apple or Google Play.

1.5 Voice recordings (Tone Match feature)

If you use the "Tone Match" pronunciation practice feature, your microphone audio is analyzed entirely on your device. Voice recordings are never uploaded to us or to any third-party server, and are not stored after analysis completes.

1.6 Voice recordings (Pronunciation Practice feature)

Separately from Tone Match, our Pronunciation Practice feature uses your device's built-in speech recognition (provided by Apple on iOS or Google on Android) to check how closely your spoken words match the target pronunciation. Depending on your device and operating system settings, this may involve your voice recording being processed by Apple's or Google's speech recognition service, which operates under its own privacy policy. We do not store these recordings on our own servers, and the recording is only used to generate a pronunciation score.

1.7 Advertising data (free-tier users)

Free-tier (non-Premium) users may see ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, or Android Advertising ID) to serve and measure ads. VietQuest requests non-personalized ads only, so your advertising identifier is not used to personalize ads. On iOS, the App may show Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt; whichever choice you make, you will still see the same non-personalized ads. If you told us you're under 13 (see 1.2), ads are always non-personalized and content-restricted, and this identifier is not used to personalize ads regardless of any other setting. Premium subscribers never see ads and this data is never collected from them.

We do not use Facebook SDK or Google Analytics. The only analytics we use are the usage events described in section 1.10.

1.8 Automatically collected technical data

Like most online services, our service providers (Google Firebase, Google AdMob, RevenueCat, Sentry) automatically record certain technical information in server logs when the App communicates with their servers — such as IP address, device/OS type, and app version. This is standard technical logging used for operating, securing, and troubleshooting the service, and is not used to build an advertising profile of you beyond what is described in section 1.7. For signed-in users, Firebase App Check may also send device-integrity signals to Google so that our database only accepts requests from genuine copies of the App.

1.9 Crash and error data

If the App encounters an unexpected error, technical diagnostic information (device type, OS version, app version, and the error details/stack trace) is automatically sent to our crash-reporting provider, Sentry, so we can identify and fix bugs. This diagnostic data never includes your personal messages, voice recordings, or learning content.

1.10 Usage events (signed-in users only)

If you are signed in, VietQuest records basic usage events, such as which lesson was completed or skipped, using fixed event names and simple values such as a level number. They are stored in our Google Firestore database together with your account ID, so we can see which lessons are too hard or too long. These events never contain free text, voice recordings or your answers. If you do not sign in, no usage events are recorded.

2. How We Use Information

  • To screen, on your device only, whether an account and personalized ads should be offered to you at all (see 1.2).
  • To let signed-in users back up and restore their learning progress across devices (Premium feature).
  • To operate the Leaderboard, the referral program (including paying gem rewards and preventing duplicate or abusive claims), and the multi-device sign-in limit.
  • To verify and manage your Premium subscription status.
  • To operate and measure the AdMob advertising shown to free-tier users.
  • To identify, diagnose, and fix technical errors and crashes.
  • To understand, from signed-in usage events, which lessons are too hard or too long (see 1.10).
  • To provide customer support when you contact us or submit feedback.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone, and we do not use your learning progress or voice data for advertising purposes.

3. Who We Share Data With

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore) — our cloud infrastructure provider for account sign-in, progress sync, leaderboard, referral data, and usage events.
  • RevenueCat — our subscription management provider, which receives your account ID and purchase/subscription status to manage Premium access.
  • Google AdMob — our advertising provider for free-tier users, subject to Google's own privacy policy.
  • Sentry — our crash and error reporting provider, which receives technical diagnostic data (see 1.9) when the App encounters an unexpected error.
  • Apple / Google Play — for processing subscription payments; VietQuest itself never sees or stores your payment card details.

We do not share your data with any other third party, and we do not sell it.

3.1 International data transfer

Our service providers (Google Firebase, Google AdMob, RevenueCat, Sentry) may process and store data on servers located outside your country of residence, including in the United States or other countries where they operate data centers. By using the App, you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in countries that may have different data protection laws than your own.

3.2 Users in Vietnam

If you are located in Vietnam, your personal data is processed in accordance with Decree 13/2023/NĐ-CP on Personal Data Protection in addition to the practices described throughout this Policy. By creating an account (section 1.3), you consent to the collection, processing, and — as described in section 3.1 — cross-border transfer of your personal data for the purposes described in section 2. You may withdraw this consent at any time by deleting your account (section 4.1) or by contacting us at the email in section 13, and you may exercise the other rights listed in section 4 in the same way.

4. Data Retention, Deletion, and Your Rights

Locally stored data remains on your device until you delete the app. Cloud-synced data remains linked to your account until you delete your account or ask us to delete it.

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you (your synced progress and account email).
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Delete your account and its associated data.
  • Withdraw consent to cloud sync at any time by signing out in Settings.

4.1 How to Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your VietQuest account at any time, directly within the app:

  1. Open the VietQuest app
  2. Tap the Settings icon (top-right of the Home screen)
  3. In the Account section, tap Delete account
  4. Confirm the deletion

This immediately and permanently deletes from our systems: your sign-in account and email address, learning progress, streak history, gem balance, your Leaderboard entries (current week and previous weeks), the feedback messages you submitted, your usage events (see 1.10), your referral code and your list of invited friends, and your registered devices. This action cannot be undone.

To prevent fraud (for example claiming a referral reward again with a new account) and to keep billing records accurate, a few small technical records are kept after deletion, linked only to your former anonymous account ID and containing no email address, name or nickname: the record of gems already granted, the referral reward-paid markers, a Leaderboard rate-limit marker, and your subscription entitlement record. Once your sign-in account is deleted, these cannot be used to sign in or to identify you from the App. If you would like them removed as well, email us at the address in section 13 and we will delete them by hand, typically within a few business days. Purchase records held by Apple, Google or RevenueCat follow their own policies.

For security, deleting an account may ask you to sign in again first. If you registered with an email and password and signed in a long time ago, sign out, sign back in, and then delete the account.

To exercise any other rights above, email us at the address in section 13 — we will respond and act on your request within a reasonable time.

5. Children's Privacy

VietQuest is not directed or marketed to children under the age of 13. As described in section 1.2, the App asks every new user what year they were born and automatically disables account creation/sign-in and switches to child-directed, non-personalized advertising for anyone who tells us they are under 13 — we do not knowingly collect email addresses, nicknames, or other account data from a user who tells us they are under 13. This screening relies on what you tell us; it cannot independently verify your age. Accounts and Premium subscriptions are intended to be created and managed by an adult. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child under 13 without appropriate consent, we will delete it promptly. If you believe this has happened, please contact us at the email below.

6. Security

We use industry-standard practices (including Firebase Security Rules and encrypted connections) to protect the data we hold. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices

  • You can use most of VietQuest's features entirely offline and without creating an account.
  • You can sign out at any time in Settings.
  • You can delete your account at any time in Settings (see 4.1).
  • On iOS, you can change your App Tracking Transparency permission at any time in your device's Settings app.

8. Advertising Consent

The free version of VietQuest shows ads from Google AdMob. The App requests non-personalized ads only. On iOS, the App may also show Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, and you can change that choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you first see a consent form from Google where you can accept or refuse the use of data for ads. Even for non-personalized ads, AdMob may use device identifiers to deliver ads, limit how often you see them, measure results and prevent fraud.

  • Legitimate interests — to keep the App secure, prevent abuse, fix crashes and understand which lessons work.
  • Consent — for advertising choices (the consent form in section 8) and for using your microphone. You can withdraw consent at any time in the consent form or in your device settings.

You have the right to access, correct, delete and export your data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Our service providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under the safeguards described in their own privacy policies (for example standard contractual clauses). To use a right, email us at the address in section 13.

10. California Residents

California residents may ask what personal information we hold about them, ask us to delete or correct it, and will not be treated differently for using these rights. We do not collect sensitive personal information. To make a request, email us at the address in section 13.

11. Asking Us to Delete Your Data Without the App

If you still have the App, use Settings > Account > Delete account (see section 4.1). If you have uninstalled the App or cannot sign in, email support.vietquest@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my VietQuest data", from the email address linked to your account (or tell us the nickname you used). We will confirm your request and delete your account and cloud data within 30 days. Technical records that the in-app deletion keeps (see section 4.1) are deleted by hand on request. Purchase records held by Apple or Google follow their own policies.
