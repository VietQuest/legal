Help for VietQuest: Learn Vietnamese on iPhone and Android.

Contact us

Questions, problems or feedback? Email us and we will get back to you as soon as we can:

support.vietquest@gmail.com

To help us solve a problem faster, please include your device model, your iOS or Android version, the app version (shown in Settings), and a short description of what happened.

You can also send feedback from inside the app using the Feedback form.

Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel my Premium subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple (iPhone, iPad) or Google (Android), not by us.

  • iPhone / iPad: open Settings, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions, choose VietQuest and tap Cancel Subscription.
  • Android: open the Google Play Store, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions, choose VietQuest and tap Cancel subscription.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid being charged for the next one. Deleting the app or your VietQuest account does not cancel a subscription.

I was charged and want a refund.

All purchases are processed by Apple (iPhone, iPad) or Google (Android), and we cannot issue refunds ourselves. Please request a refund directly from them: for App Store purchases, go to reportaproblem.apple.com; for Google Play purchases, request it through Google Play.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app, go to Settings > Delete account. If you have uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, see Asking Us to Delete Your Data Without the App in our Privacy Policy, or email us at the address above with the subject "Delete my VietQuest data".

My progress is missing or does not match on another device.

Syncing progress across devices is a Premium feature and requires signing in to the same account on each device. If something still looks wrong, email us with the details above.

The microphone or speaking practice does not work.

Check that VietQuest has permission to use the microphone (and speech recognition on iPhone) in your device Settings, then reopen the app.
